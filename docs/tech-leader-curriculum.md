## What the board agreed on

Six independent seats, arguing different corners. They converged on four things.

**1. This is not a knowledge gap.** Five of six seats said it unprompted, in almost the same words. You are not under-read. You lead 150 people across three EGMs, own a 30-month greenfield build and a 460-app estate, and ship production software on weekends. Adding content to that is not the intervention.

**2. The Masters in Data Science and AI should be killed — 6/6.** Not deferred. Killed, in writing, this month. The reasoning stacked up from every direction:

- *Kingmaker:* "Never once in 200 placements has a Masters broken a tie at EGM level."
- *Operator:* "It signals 'still becoming technical' precisely when you need to signal 'already commercial.' The ambiguity is costing you more than the course would give."
- *Architect:* "Two years to be taught gradient descent by someone who has shipped less than you have."
- *Practitioner:* "Your own system has been telling you it's dead. Believe it."
- *Behavioural Designer:* "Externally gated, high activation energy, documented multi-month zero-signal history. Do not build a year on the one commitment with proven negative evidence."
- *Steward:* "An identity purchase, not a capability need."

This contradicts a stated TELOS long-term goal, so it is your call, not theirs. But the board's view was unanimous and the evidence is your own: *"Masters: 1x / 1 context in 7d. Zero appearances in 24h."* Give it one 20-minute session in week one — enrol or kill. Either outcome ends the ambient guilt that is currently taxing every other slot.

**3. The spine is artefacts shipped to named humans, not books read.** The Behavioural Designer's diagnosis is the load-bearing insight of the whole board:

> *"Every other priority in your life is attached to a cue, a person, or a mat with someone trying to choke you. Study is attached to nothing."*
>
> *"Measurement is not a contingency. Reading 'Masters: 1x in 7d' produces the appropriate discomfort, and the feeling of having a system that's on it substitutes for the act. Your instrumentation is unusually good at supplying that."*

You have world-class telemetry and a chronic execution gap. More telemetry makes it worse. What you have zero of, and what your dashboard cannot manufacture, is **something due to a person you respect, on a date.**

**4. The build compulsion is the delivery mechanism, not the enemy — with one hard boundary.** PAI/Sage at 236 mentions in 24 hours against Masters at 5 in 7 days is not weakness, it is arithmetic. Building returns a compile-run-works loop in 90 seconds on a variable schedule — the most reinforcing pattern known to behavioural science. Study returns nothing for six weeks. Willpower does not win that matchup.

So the curriculum borrows the loop. Every quarter produces a built or written artefact. But: **no building study infrastructure.** No SRS app, no curriculum tracker, no reading-list agent, no ISA spec for the syllabus. That is the displacement failure wearing a lab coat. If you build the tool, you have already lost the year.

## Where the board split — and how it resolves

**The Architect vs. The Steward** was the real fight.

*Architect:* your genuine gaps are systems-level and will detonate on TMS — distributed failure modes, data contracts, strangulation as technique not slogan, evaluating AI systems, unit economics. Build your way through them.

*Steward:* deep technical study "feeds the precise pathology killing you — the need to be the smartest builder in the room. At EGM your job is to stop building. This is your comfort, not your gap."

*Kingmaker and Operator sided with the Steward:* "technical mastery has stopped being your moat and started being your hiding place"; "building is where you go when the political work is uncomfortable — that is the plateau mechanism, exactly."

**The ruling:** the Steward's 90-day PAI/Sage feature freeze stands. But the reclaimed hours do not go to nothing — they go to **work-facing** builds only. `tms-sim` and a failure register serve Australia Post and your board case. A new Sage feature serves your nervous system. That distinction is the whole boundary.

The Steward's residual objection stands on the record: even work-facing building can be escape. Here is the test he gave, and it is a good one — **log the hour you build.** Post-conflict, post-ambiguity, after 9pm is regulation, not craft. Two corroborating checks: does anyone other than you depend on it, and can you name one week in six months where you built nothing and were fine.

**The second split — "earned, not chased."** The Kingmaker and Operator both refused to let you hide behind it:

> *"Humility that withholds information from decision-makers is not integrity. It is a tax on your own team."* — The Operator
>
> *"Earning it means delivering. Being chosen means someone must know what you delivered. You are not selling yourself — you are giving your sponsor ammunition."*

This is the hardest item in the document. Sit with it rather than filing it.

## The honest time budget

**3 × 25 minutes — Tue/Wed/Thu, between prayer and training.**
**1 × 90 minutes — Saturday 07:00–08:30.**
**≈165 min/week ≈ 130 hours/year.**

That is a genuine postgraduate load if none of it is wasted on passive input. It is also all you have. Any plan asking for more is lying to you.

**Placement is deliberate.** Not before Bible study — never touch the load-bearing wall. Not in the evening: evenings belong to Vanya, and a recently-relocated man doing BJJ and CrossFit has no cognitive evening. Not Sunday.

Study sits immediately *after* your strongest existing cue and immediately *before* a reward you will not skip. Training becomes the reinforcer for study.

**The implementation intention, written verbatim:**

> *"After I close my Bible and before I put my shoes on, I open ONE file and work 25 minutes."*

Not "study more." Nouns don't fire. Every other priority in your stack is a verb with a location — *open Bible, tie belt, pick up camera.* This one needs to be too.

# The Curriculum

Four quarters. Each produces **one flagship artefact, shipped to a named human, on a date.** Reading is ammunition for the artefact, never the point.

---

## Q1 · Aug–Oct 2026 — **Commercial Credibility**

*The highest-ROI quarter, per both the Kingmaker and the Operator.*

**Flagship artefact: TMS Benefits Realisation Baseline (2 pages).**

Rebuild the approved TMS board case yourself, in a spreadsheet, from first principles. Specifically:

- Separate capex from opex, and know why it matters — capitalised build flatters current-year EBIT and creates a depreciation drag from go-live. That is the cliff nobody has modelled on a 30-month programme.
- Tag every claimed benefit: **cashable / cost-avoidance / fiction.**
- Name a benefit owner for each — *outside IT.*
- Identify cost-to-serve per parcel and exactly where TMS moves it.

**Ship to:** the CFO's finance business partner, and ask them to tear it apart. Then McNamara.

**Reading:** Berman & Knight, *Financial Intelligence*. Australia Post's last three annual reports, line by line — segment reporting, CSO cost disclosure, capex profile. Flyvbjerg, *How Big Things Get Done* (the only book with real data on why megaprojects fail; reference-class forecasting applies directly to your phased pilot rollout).

**Arena rep:** take one carrier negotiation yourself. Carrier onboarding is commercial and it is your only available commercial rep. You are currently missing it.

## Q2 · Nov 2026–Jan 2027 — **The Narrative**

**Flagship artefact: the ToM six-pager.**

Prose. No slides. Six pages. Written by you, alone, in the protected morning block. No consultant, no deck team, no working group. Then signed by McNamara, Plant and Bannister.

This is already an FY26 goal. The Practitioner called it your FY26 goal, your alignment mechanism, your succession tool and your EGM audition simultaneously — *"if you cannot do that, no curriculum on earth will help you."*

**Secondary artefact: Operation Daylight board paper (1 page).** Decision sought, options rejected and why, risk, cost, what happens if we do nothing. Papers get deferred when they present analysis instead of a decision, hide the downside, or arrive un-pre-briefed.

Rehearse the hostile question in 40 seconds: *"460 shadow applications — how would we know if a breach had already happened?"*

**Ship to:** all three EGMs. Separately, take the 460 apps to the CRO **before** it becomes an incident, not after.

**Reading:** Heifetz & Linsky, *Leadership on the Line* (the chapter on hungers). ANAO reports on federal IT programmes. **The Post Office / Horizon inquiry findings** — required reading on how technology programmes destroy executives in postal organisations.

**Arena rep:** once this quarter, pre-brief all three EGMs and then still present a recommendation you know one of them will hate — and hold it. *"Never surprise" must not become "never contest."*

## Q3 · Feb–Apr 2027 — **Systems Judgement Under Failure**

*Where the build compulsion is legitimately spent.*

**Flagship artefact: TMS Failure Register + pre-mortem dated 2028.**

Thirty failure modes across the consignment lifecycle: detection, blast radius, mitigation, named owner. Plus the pre-mortem written as *"it failed, here is why."*

Optionally backed by `tms-sim` — a discrete-event simulation of the transport network in TypeScript: depots, carriers, consignment state machine, injected faults (carrier API timeouts, duplicate scans, clock skew, partial batch failure). Run game days against it.

The Architect's point stands: your personal systems are single-user and fail silently at 3am with no consequence. TMS has retry storms, gray failure, correlated carrier-API timeouts and backpressure across 460 upstreams. A parcel scanned twice is not an idempotency abstraction — it is a truck going to the wrong depot.

**Ship to:** Howard Grace and the TMS steerco. This artefact alone justifies your seat at the board case review — it makes you the only person in the room who can say *"here is what breaks, here is when, here is what it costs."*

**Reading:** Kleppmann, *Designing Data-Intensive Applications* — **chapters 5–9 only.** Huang et al., *Gray Failure* (HotOS '17). Bronson et al., *Metastable Failures in Distributed Systems* (HotOS '21). Dean & Barroso, *The Tail at Scale*. Fowler, *StranglerFigApplication*. Andrew Jones, *Driving Data Quality with Data Contracts*.

**Also this quarter, one page:** a unit-cost model — cost per parcel, per scan, per carrier onboarded — you can put in front of three EGMs.

**Arena rep:** personally run the post-incident review on the worst P1 of the year, in front of the affected EGM, without a comms filter.

## Q4 · May–Jul 2027 — **Leverage and Succession**

**Flagship artefact: succession memos.**

One page per direct report: what they need to be ready in 24 months. Three named successors, each with a stretch assignment and a date. Hand one of them a budget line and a board presentation.

Three seats made the same argument independently: **you cannot be promoted out of a role no one else can hold.** Your stated legacy goal — *"direct reports who go on to lead well themselves"* — is also, by some distance, your single strongest career move. Search consultants ask *"who did you develop?"* and then ring them.

**Secondary artefact: the value-creation record (2 pages).** Not a CV. Quantified: TMS business case value, Front Door consolidation savings across 460+ apps, P1/P2 SLA delta, cost-to-budget variance. Plus six named referees who will take a cold call.

**Reading:** Marquet, *Turn the Ship Around* (intent-based leadership, aimed squarely at the legacy goal). Charan, Drotter & Noel, *The Leadership Pipeline* — the function-leader → business-leader transition you are currently stalled inside. Smart & Street, *Who*.

**External moves this quarter:** one coffee each with three ANZ tech-exec search partners (Egon Zehnder, Derwent, Blenheim Partners) — no role in play, one question: *"I want an EGM seat in 24 months, tell me what's missing."* And one real speaking slot: Gartner Symposium ANZ, an ADAPT forum, or a supply-chain/public-sector CIO forum. **One.** Not LinkedIn posting.

## The Continuous Track — Formation

Runs underneath all twelve months. Not a quarter, not optional. The Steward's position, which no other seat contested:

> *"You built a system that measures everything, and the one thing it cannot see is you."*

**Daily**
- Fixed morning office. 25 minutes, same chair, same time. Scripture and prayer. Phone in another room.
- Examen, 5 minutes, nightly.

**Weekly**
- **Sabbath: 24 hours, phone in a drawer.** Vanya keeps the record, not you.
- 90 minutes with Vanya, fixed, "state of us." Half governance, half not.
- Fortnightly: teach one concept from the current quarter to your leadership team. Ten minutes, unscripted, no slides. Highest-yield retrieval practice in existence, costs zero new hours, and directly serves the legacy goal.

**Monthly**
- A spiritual director — someone unimpressed by your title.
- An executive coach — a *different* person.
- An external mentor who has actually held an EGM seat. Their job is the political read, not the technical one.

**First 90 days**
- **Hogan Development Survey** — it measures derailers directly (bold/hubris, diligent-perfectionism, mischievous).
- A real 360 from all three EGMs and four directs.
- Kegan & Lahey's *Immunity to Change*: map one competing commitment. The Steward called this the hardest technical work of the year, and he is probably right.

**Two silent 3-day retreats.** Phone surrendered. First within 60 days.

**A written Rule of Life.** One page, drafted with Vanya, reviewed quarterly.

**Formation texts — six across the year, one per two months.** Each answered in writing to a peer, not summarised:
Nouwen, *In the Name of Jesus* (the temptations to be relevant, spectacular, powerful) · Barton, *Strengthening the Soul of Your Leadership* · Peterson, *A Long Obedience in the Same Direction* · Heschel, *The Sabbath* · Keller, *The Meaning of Marriage* · Palmer, *Let Your Life Speak*

**The marriage is in the curriculum, not adjacent to it.** Quarterly, Vanya scores your presence 1–10 and **that number is the programme's primary KPI.** Your self-report is inadmissible. Any new commitment over two hours a week requires her explicit written yes.

## Tripwires and recovery

Designed on the assumption that you *will* miss. Missing is not the failure — the restart is the habit.

1. **Two consecutive missed sessions** → floor protocol within 48 hours: 10 minutes, one page, one paragraph written, in the normal slot. **Never three.**
2. **21 days with no artefact shipped to a human** → this is the real failure. Sessions are input; artefacts are the system.
3. **The displacement signature:** PAI commits > 20 in a week while study sessions = 0. This is the **one** number Sage should surface. Nothing else.
4. **Never catch up.** Catching up is why people quit. Say this out loud in month one so it is pre-decided rather than negotiated in a bad week.
5. **Recovery red/yellow** → the first thing that drops is *building*. Never sleep, never sabbath, never Vanya.

**Sage's role, and its limits.** Hold the four artefact ship-dates. Surface the displacement signature. That is all. Explicitly **not** more nudges: every suggested action you declined taught the system that its nudges carry no cost. That channel is burnt. Adding alerts makes it worse.

**The monthly review already exists.** Add exactly two questions: *Did the artefact ship? What did I change at work because of it?*

## The test at twelve months

Not *"did he read the books."* Five answers, three of which you do not get to see first.

1. **Vanya** on *"are you more present than a year ago?"* — the primary KPI.
2. **Your directs** on *"is he easier to disagree with?"*
3. **The sabbath ledger** Vanya keeps.
4. **Four artefacts shipped and signed** — the benefits baseline, the ToM six-pager, the failure register, the succession memos.
5. **One named person has said "yes, I'll back you."**

The Steward's closing line is the one worth keeping:

> *"If those come back right, you'll be ready for EGM. If they don't, the title will arrive and you won't be there to receive it."*
