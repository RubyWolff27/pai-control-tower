## What the board agreed on

**1. This is not a knowledge gap.** Five of six seats said it unprompted, in almost the same words.

**2. The Masters in Data Science and AI should be killed — 6/6.** Not deferred. Killed, in writing, this month.

- *Kingmaker:* "Never once in 200 placements has a Masters broken a tie at EGM level."
- *Operator:* "It signals 'still becoming technical' precisely when you need to signal 'already commercial.' The ambiguity is costing you more than the course would give."
- *Architect:* "Two years to be taught gradient descent by someone who has shipped less than you have."
- *Practitioner:* "Your own system has been telling you it's dead. Believe it."
- *Behavioural Designer:* "Externally gated, high activation energy, documented multi-month zero-signal history."
- *Steward:* "An identity purchase, not a capability need."

This contradicts a stated TELOS long-term goal, so it is your call, not theirs. Give it one 20-minute session in week one — enrol or kill. Either outcome ends the ambient guilt currently taxing every other slot.

**3. The spine is artefacts shipped to named humans, not books read.**

> *"Every other priority in your life is attached to a cue, a person, or a mat with someone trying to choke you. Study is attached to nothing."*
>
> *"Measurement is not a contingency. Reading 'Masters: 1x in 7d' produces the appropriate discomfort, and the feeling of having a system that's on it substitutes for the act."*

**4. The build compulsion is the delivery mechanism — with one hard boundary.** PAI/Sage at 236 mentions in 24 hours against Masters at 5 in 7 days is arithmetic, not weakness. Building returns a compile-run-works loop in 90 seconds on a variable schedule. Study returns nothing for six weeks. So the curriculum borrows the loop — but **no building study infrastructure.** No SRS app, no curriculum tracker, no reading-list agent, no ISA spec for the syllabus. If you build the tool, you have already lost the year.

## Where the board split — and how it resolves

**Architect vs. Steward** was the real fight. Architect: your gaps are systems-level and will detonate on TMS. Steward: deep technical study "feeds the precise pathology killing you — the need to be the smartest builder in the room." Kingmaker and Operator sided with the Steward.

**The ruling:** the 90-day PAI/Sage feature freeze stands, but reclaimed hours go to **work-facing** builds only. `tms-sim` serves Australia Post. A new Sage feature serves your nervous system.

**The Steward's test, left on the record:** log the *hour* you build. Post-conflict, post-ambiguity, after 9pm is regulation, not craft. Two corroborating checks — does anyone other than you depend on it, and can you name one week in six months where you built nothing and were fine.

**The second split — "earned, not chased."** Both business seats refused to let you hide behind it:

> *"Humility that withholds information from decision-makers is not integrity. It is a tax on your own team. You are not selling yourself — you are giving your sponsor ammunition."*

## The honest time budget

**3 × 25 min — Tue/Wed/Thu, between prayer and training** · **1 × 90 min — Saturday 07:00–08:30**
**≈165 min/week ≈ 130 hours/year.** That is all you have. Any plan asking for more is lying to you.

Placement is deliberate: not before Bible study (never touch the load-bearing wall), not evenings (they belong to Vanya), not Sunday. Study sits after your strongest existing cue and before a reward you will not skip — training becomes the reinforcer.

**The implementation intention, verbatim:**
> *"After I close my Bible and before I put my shoes on, I open ONE file and work 25 minutes."*

Nouns don't fire. Every other priority in your stack is a verb with a location — *open Bible, tie belt, pick up camera.* This one needs to be too.

# The Curriculum

Four quarters. Each produces **one flagship artefact, shipped to a named human, on a date.** Reading is ammunition for the artefact, never the point.

## Q1 · Aug–Oct 2026 — Commercial Credibility

*The highest-ROI quarter, per both the Kingmaker and the Operator.*

**Flagship artefact: TMS Benefits Realisation Baseline (2 pages).**

Rebuild the approved TMS board case yourself, in a spreadsheet, from first principles:

- Separate capex from opex and know why it matters — capitalised build flatters current-year EBIT and creates a depreciation drag from go-live. That is the cliff nobody has modelled on a 30-month programme.
- Tag every claimed benefit: **cashable / cost-avoidance / fiction.**
- Name a benefit owner for each — *outside IT.*
- Identify cost-to-serve per parcel and where TMS actually moves it.

**Ship to:** the CFO's finance business partner — ask them to tear it apart. Then McNamara.

Prose. No slides. Six pages. Written by you, alone, in the protected morning block. No consultant, no deck team, no working group. Then signed by McNamara, Plant and Bannister.

> *"This is your FY26 goal, your alignment mechanism, your succession tool and your EGM audition simultaneously. If you cannot do that, no curriculum on earth will help you."* — The Practitioner

**Ship to:** all three EGMs. Separately, take the 460 apps to the CRO **before** it becomes an incident.

## Q3 · Feb–Apr 2027 — Systems Judgement Under Failure

*Where the build compulsion is legitimately spent.*

**Flagship artefact: TMS Failure Register + pre-mortem dated 2028.**

Thirty failure modes across the consignment lifecycle: detection, blast radius, mitigation, named owner. Plus the pre-mortem written as *"it failed, here is why."*

Optionally backed by `tms-sim` — a discrete-event simulation in TypeScript: depots, carriers, consignment state machine, injected faults (carrier API timeouts, duplicate scans, clock skew, partial batch failure). Run game days against it.

> *"Your personal systems are single-user and fail silently at 3am with no consequence. A parcel scanned twice is not an idempotency abstraction — it is a truck going to the wrong depot."* — The Architect

**Ship to:** Howard Grace and the TMS steerco. This makes you the only person in the room who can say *"here is what breaks, here is when, here is what it costs."*

## Q4 · May–Jul 2027 — Leverage and Succession

**Flagship artefact: succession memos.**

One page per direct report: what they need to be ready in 24 months. Three named successors, each with a stretch assignment and a date. Hand one of them a budget line and a board presentation.

Three seats made the same argument independently: **you cannot be promoted out of a role no one else can hold.** Your stated legacy goal is also, by some distance, your strongest career move. Search consultants ask *"who did you develop?"* — and then ring them.

**Secondary artefact: the value-creation record (2 pages).** Not a CV. Quantified: TMS business case value, Front Door consolidation savings across 460+ apps, P1/P2 SLA delta, cost-to-budget variance. Plus six named referees who will take a cold call.

## The Continuous Track — Formation

Runs underneath all twelve months. Not a quarter, not optional.

> *"You built a system that measures everything, and the one thing it cannot see is you."* — The Steward

**Daily** — Fixed morning office, 25 min, same chair, same time. Scripture and prayer. Phone in another room. Examen, 5 min, nightly.

**Weekly** — **Sabbath: 24 hours, phone in a drawer.** Vanya keeps the record, not you. 90 minutes with Vanya, fixed, "state of us." Half governance, half not.

**Fortnightly** — Teach one concept from the current quarter to your leadership team. Ten minutes, unscripted, no slides. Highest-yield retrieval practice in existence, costs zero new hours, and directly serves the legacy goal.

**Monthly** — A spiritual director (someone unimpressed by your title). An executive coach — a *different* person. An external mentor who has held an EGM seat; their job is the political read, not the technical one.

## Tripwires and recovery

Designed on the assumption that you *will* miss. Missing is not the failure — the restart is the habit.

1. **Two consecutive missed sessions** → floor protocol within 48 hours: 10 minutes, one page, one paragraph written, in the normal slot. **Never three.**
2. **21 days with no artefact shipped to a human** → the real failure. Sessions are input; artefacts are the system.
3. **The displacement signature:** PAI commits > 20 in a week while study sessions = 0. This is the **one** number Sage should surface. Nothing else.
4. **Never catch up.** Catching up is why people quit. Say this out loud in month one so it is pre-decided rather than negotiated in a bad week.
5. **Recovery red/yellow** → the first thing that drops is *building*. Never sleep, never sabbath, never Vanya.

**Sage's role, and its limits.** Hold the four artefact ship-dates. Surface the displacement signature. That is all. Explicitly **not** more nudges: every suggested action you declined taught the system that its nudges carry no cost. That channel is burnt.

**The monthly review already exists.** Add exactly two questions: *Did the artefact ship? What did I change at work because of it?*

# Continuous Learning — Year Two and Beyond

The twelve months above is a programme. What follows is an operating system. The point of the first year is to install a cadence you never have to re-decide.

## The permanent cadence

| Rhythm | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Quarterly** | **One artefact, shipped to a named human, on a date.** Forever. This is the whole system. Rotate the lens — commercial, narrative, systems, people — but never drop to zero. |
| **Fortnightly** | Teach one thing to your leadership team. Ten minutes, no slides. Never stops. |
| **Monthly** | Spiritual director · executive coach · external mentor. Three separate people, three different jobs. |
| **Annually** | Re-baseline TELOS against actual behavioural data, not intentions. Refresh the value-creation record whether or not you are looking. |

## Standing inputs — the permanent reading diet

These renew themselves; you never need a new reading list again.

**Every August — the organisation's own numbers.** [Australia Post's Annual Report](https://auspost.com.au/about-us/news-media/publications) and the updated [Statement of Corporate Intent](https://auspost.com.au/content/dam/auspost_corp/media/documents/statement-of-corporate-intent-2025-26-to-2028-29.pdf). Read them the week they drop, before anyone briefs you on them. Two hours. Compounds more than any book.

**Every year — the audit and accountability layer.** The [ANAO Annual Audit Work Program](https://www.anao.gov.au/work-program/overview) tells you what will be scrutinised next. [ANAO performance audits](https://www.anao.gov.au/pubs/performance-audit) tell you how comparable programmes actually failed. This is free, specific, and almost nobody at your level reads it.

**Continuously — engineering judgement.**
- [AWS Builders' Library](https://aws.amazon.com/builders-library/) — timeouts, retries, jitter, backpressure. Short, practical, permanent.
- [ACM Queue](https://queue.acm.org/) — the practitioners' journal; one article a month.
- [Martin Fowler's site](https://martinfowler.com/) — the legacy-modernisation tag stays relevant for the whole Daylight arc.
- The [DORA State of DevOps report](https://dora.dev/), annually — the only credible measurement instrument for "velocity high," which is currently a vibe.

**Continuously — the outside view.** One serious industry publication read weekly rather than five skimmed. Rotate every couple of years so you don't calcify.

## Skill decay — what to refresh, and when

| Every | What |
|---|---|
| **1 year** | Financial fluency, on live material. Rebuild one business case from first principles annually — a different one each time. It decays fast and it is the thing that gets tested. |
| **2 years** | A 360 with fresh raters, and a re-run of the Hogan derailer profile. Derailers move under load. |
| **2–3 years** | Technical currency in one area you have started hand-waving about. Pick it by noticing where you have stopped asking questions. |
| **3–5 years** | The peer network. Relationships that go unrefreshed for five years are archaeology, not a network. |

## The permanent board

The six seats that debated this were a device, but the structure is worth keeping as real people:

- **A commercial voice** — CFO or a finance director who will tell you your numbers are wrong.
- **A technical peer** — someone still building, who can tell you when you are hand-waving.
- **A political read** — an external mentor who has held an EGM seat.
- **A formation voice** — the spiritual director.
- **A truth-teller from below** — a direct report who is not frightened of you.
- **Vanya** — with the veto.

Refresh the composition every two to three years. Anyone who has stopped disagreeing with you has stopped being useful.

## What the board cut — permanently

- **The Masters in Data Science and AI** — 6/6, kill it
- **The LinkedIn Learning agile/sprint course** — stale since March; delete
- **Vendor certifications** (AWS SA Pro et al.) — pure signalling for someone with a shipping record
- **A twelve-book reading list** — ~50 passive hours against a 130-hour budget, dead by week six. Four books read twice with written retrieval beats twelve read once. Every time.
- **Generic executive-education / MBA modules** — content is downstream of what you already do. If you go, go for the cohort; be honest that the cohort is the entire product.
- **Any evening study block** — fantasy. Cut on sight.
- **New Sage/PAI features for 90 days** — the freeze *is* the intervention.
